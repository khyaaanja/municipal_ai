# Brag Plan: भक्तपुर AI — cinematic product film

## What is this app?
A bilingual civic AI for Bhaktapur Municipality: a citizen speaks to their phone in
Nepali, the AI understands the problem, finds the location, files the municipal report,
tracks it to resolution, and guides service applications end to end.

## The angle
The user supplied `ai1.png` — a finished nine-panel Nepali storyboard set in Bhaktapur,
with Newari architecture, the glowing AI face, real reference IDs (SL-45872,
BC-2026-00456) and a भक्तपुर AI hero banner. **That storyboard is the film.**

What I cannot do is generate new 3D character animation — Hyperframes renders HTML, not
a character rig, and inventing a different-looking CG citizen would break continuity with
the art that already exists. What I can do is what an editor does with finished frames:
**move the camera through them.** Each panel gets a slow push or drift, a blurred
full-bleed fill of itself behind a sharp inset card (so the frame is full-colour without
showing the upscale), and live animated overlays on top — AI waveform, checkmarks landing,
step markers advancing, glow pulses — in the blue/purple AI palette the brief asks for.

The result is a product film built from the user's own art, not a slideshow of it.

## Hook (first 3 seconds)
Panel 1, pushed in close on the citizen's face mid-sentence, the Bhaktapur street soft
behind him. His line rises as live text: **"यो सडकको बत्ती बिग्रिएको छ।"** No UI, no logo,
no product — a person with a problem. The product does not appear until he speaks.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **AI ले बुझ्छ** — the waveform animates live over the panel, and the three checks land
  one at a time: समस्या पहिचान · स्थान पहिचान · विवरण संकलन
- **रिपोर्ट तयार भयो** — `SL-45872` resolves digit by digit, the send button pulses
- **समाधानको यात्रा** — four status rows advance with their real timestamps, ending on
  समस्या समाधान भयो, cut against the repaired street light at night
- **सेवा आवेदन → आवेदन पेश भयो** — the second citizen, the जन्मदर्ता flow, `BC-2026-00456`

## Outro / punchline
The hero banner, revealed as a slow horizontal drift across the Bhaktapur skyline:
**भक्तपुर AI** · *नेपालको आफ्नै AI सिटी असिस्टेन्ट* · **"भन्नुहोस्! AI ले गरिदिन्छ!"**
then the four audience tiles, then the company signature.

## User flow worth showing
Exactly the brief's progression, and exactly what the panels contain:
**PROBLEM → VOICE → AI UNDERSTANDS → ACTION → TRACKING → SOLUTION**, then the same arc
again for a service application.

## Tone
- Preset: `cinematic`
- Creative direction: *Apple-style product film, Nepali, built from the supplied storyboard*
- Interpretation: Slow camera, long holds, no hard cuts — every transition is a dissolve
  with scale. Restraint in overlay: the art carries the emotion, the overlays carry the
  product. No excessive text, per the brief.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 56.8 seconds

## Visual identity
- Frame: `#07070C` letterbox field, panels as the light source
- **AI interface (per brief): blue/purple** — `#60A5FA` → `#A78BFA`, glow `rgba(99,102,241,.45)`
- Brand accents held back until the hero: `#EF4444` → `#F59E0B`
- Text: `#F6F1E9` · muted `#A8A29E`
- Display: **Syne** · Body: **DM Sans** · Nepali: **Noto Sans Devanagari** (all local)
- Source art: `assets/panels/p1-p8.jpg` + `hero.jpg`, cut from `ai1.png` on detected
  gutters, upscaled 2.9× lanczos + unsharp

## Share copy (draft)
भन्नुहोस्। AI ले गरिदिन्छ।
Bhaktapur AI — your city, in your own language. A product by Hashtag Technology.

## Audio direction
- Role: cinematic support — a low bed with soft interface accents, never busy
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (109.96 BPM, 117s) —
  the slowest bundled track, sitting low at ~0.24 so the picture leads
- Music cue guidance: bar = 2.183s. **Every scene boundary is on a bar line**: 6.55 /
  13.10 / 19.65 / 24.01 / 30.56 / 37.11 / 43.66 / 48.02 / 56.76. Checkmarks and status
  rows land on half-bars so they read without rushing.
- Audio-reactive: subtle — the AI glow breathes on bass during the two AI scenes only
- SFX posture: sparse, interface-flavoured — a soft activation when the AI speaks, one
  tick per checkmark, a confirmation on each reference number, one warm hit on the hero
- Restraint rule: nothing under the citizen's opening line; the first 3 seconds are
  music and picture only

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — समस्या देखियो — 0.00 → 6.55s
Panel 1. Slow push 1.06 → 1.00 on the sharp card, blurred fill drifting the other way.
Citizen's line rises bottom-left as live Devanagari. Caption: **समस्या देखियो**.
Audio intent: music only. Let the face carry it.
Transition: dissolve with scale → Scene 2.

### Scene 2 — AI ले बुझ्छ — 6.55 → 13.10s
Panel 2. The AI waveform bars animate live over the art in blue/purple; three checks land
on half-bars (8.2 / 9.3 / 10.4): समस्या पहिचान · स्थान पहिचान · विवरण संकलन. Glow breathes on bass.
Audio-coupled: soft activation as the waveform starts, one tick per check.
Transition: dissolve → Scene 3.

### Scene 3 — रिपोर्ट तयार भयो — 13.10 → 19.65s
Panel 3. **SL-45872** resolves character by character in a mono lockup beside the art;
the रिपोर्ट पठाउनुहोस् button pulses twice, then a confirmation ring completes.
Audio-coupled: confirmation tone on the ring closing.
Transition: dissolve → Scene 4.

### Scene 4 — रिपोर्ट दर्ता भयो — 19.65 → 24.01s
Panel 4. Three step markers advance left to right: रिपोर्ट प्राप्त → जाँच हुँदै → समाधान.
Short scene, one idea.
Transition: dissolve → Scene 5.

### Scene 5 — समाधानको यात्रा — 24.01 → 30.56s
Panel 5. Four status rows reveal with their real timestamps (10:24 / 11:02 / 2:15 / 5:48),
resolving on **समस्या समाधान भयो** against the repaired street light.
Audio-coupled: one soft tick per row, warmer hit on the last.
Transition: dissolve → Scene 6.

### Scene 6 — सेवा आवेदन — 30.56 → 37.11s
Panel 6. The second citizen. Her line rises: **"मलाई जन्मदर्ताको लागि आवेदन दिनु छ।"**
The AI reply follows beneath it. Slow drift, no UI clutter — this is the human beat.
Transition: dissolve → Scene 7.

### Scene 7 — आवेदन प्रक्रिया — 37.11 → 43.66s
Panel 7. Four required-information rows tick in, then three document chips land:
नागरिकता · अभिभावकको नागरिकता · अस्पतालको कागजात.
Transition: dissolve → Scene 8.

### Scene 8 — आवेदन पेश भयो — 43.66 → 48.02s
Panel 8. **आवेदन सफल भयो** with the ring completing, **BC-2026-00456** resolving.
Transition: dissolve, then the camera begins the hero drift → Scene 9.

### Scene 9 — HERO — 48.02 → 56.76s
The wide hero banner drifts slowly right to left across the Bhaktapur skyline. Over it,
in sequence: the four capability tiles, then **भक्तपुर AI**, then
*नेपालको आफ्नै AI सिटी असिस्टेन्ट*, then the tagline **"भन्नुहोस्! AI ले गरिदिन्छ!"**,
then the company line. Resolve to black.
Audio intent: the one warm swell in the film.

**Music mood:** low cinematic bed, picture-led
**Audio summary:** Three seconds of music and face, then sparse interface accents through
the two flows, one warm hit at the hero, fade.
