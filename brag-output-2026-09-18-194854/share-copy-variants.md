## English (for a wider or investor audience)
Your city, in your own language.

A citizen speaks to their phone in Nepali. The AI understands the problem, finds the
location, files the municipal report, and tracks it to resolution. The same for service
applications — birth registration, done by talking.

Bhaktapur AI — a product by Hashtag Technology.

## LinkedIn
Most civic tech asks citizens to learn the government's language: portals, forms, ward
codes, document checklists.

We built the opposite. Bhaktapur AI listens in Nepali, works out what the citizen needs,
files it with the right office, and reports back when it's done — for a broken street
light or a birth certificate.

Bhaktapur AI · नेपालको आफ्नै AI सिटी असिस्टेन्ट · a product by Hashtag Technology.
